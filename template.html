How it works

Walks come down to two things: how many batters a pitcher faces, and how often he walks one. The model estimates expected walks = batters faced × walk rate, blended with how patient the opposing lineup has been. A wild arm against a patient lineup is the sweet spot.

It outputs a real probability, so it compares straight to a price. Enter what your book offers — if the payout beats the fair price, the model sees value. Probability is driven by walk rate, batters faced and opponent patience; zone% and first-pitch strike% are context.

How it was tested. Before running live, the model was backtested — checked against games already played. For every past start it used only the stats known before that game, worked out a probability, then compared it to what actually happened. See the History tab for the results across two seasons.
